Avast Help > Privacy Policy

At Avast Antivirus, we respect and protect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, including IP addresses, browser type, and interactions with our site.
  • Device Information: Information about your device, such as operating system, type, and web browser.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance experience, analyze performance, and personalize content.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our antivirus and online protection services.
  • To respond to inquiries and deliver customer support.

  • To analyze and enhance website performance.
  • To personalize your experience with Avast.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We use encryption, secure servers, and regular audits to safeguard your information. While we take strong measures, no online transmission is 100% secure.

  • With Service Providers: For hosting, analytics, or payment processing.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Cookies help improve browsing experience and personalize content. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights
