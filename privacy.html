Information We Collect

At Avast Antivirus, we respect and protect your privacy. To provide and improve our services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, and other details you provide during registration, contact, or support interactions.
  • Usage Data: Information about your interaction with our website, products, and services, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and duration of use.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser, unique device identifiers, and system settings to ensure compatibility and enhance performance.
  • Cookies and Tracking: Used for analytics, improving website experience, remembering preferences, and personalizing content and offers.
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address or device settings to provide location-specific services.
  • Support and Communication Data: Information shared during customer support inquiries, surveys, or communications with Avast representatives.
  • Security and Threat Data: Data about malware detection, security incidents, or suspicious activity to enhance protection and maintain service quality.

We collect this information responsibly and use it only to enhance your experience, ensure security, and provide relevant services and communications.

We use your information for:

  • Delivering and improving antivirus protection and services.
  • Responding to support inquiries and requests.
  • Sending service updates, newsletters, and promotional offers.
  • Personalizing your experience on our website and apps.
  • Analyzing website and app performance to improve features.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing security threats or fraudulent activity.
  • Conducting research and analytics to better understand user needs.
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our policies.
  • Providing relevant recommendations, offers, and marketing communications.
  • Improving our communication channels and customer support efficiency.
  • Ensuring the integrity and functionality of our products and services.

We handle your data responsibly and securely, following applicable privacy laws and our Privacy Policy.

Your information is protected using:

  • Encryption of sensitive data during transmission and storage.
  • Secure servers with regular security audits and maintenance.
  • Access controls and authentication protocols for internal staff.
  • Continuous monitoring for unusual or suspicious activities.
  • Regular software updates and security patches to prevent vulnerabilities.
  • Strict data retention policies to minimize exposure of personal information.
  • Incident response protocols to quickly address potential data breaches.
  • Compliance with international security standards and regulations.
  • Employee training programs to ensure adherence to security best practices.

While we use strong measures, no online system is completely secure.

We do not sell your data. Sharing happens only under these circumstances:

  • With Service Providers: Hosting, analytics, customer support, or payment processing partners to help us provide services.
  • For Legal Compliance: When required by law, regulation, or legal process, including to protect rights and safety.
  • During Business Transfers: In the event of mergers, acquisitions, reorganizations, or asset sales.
  • With Consent: When you explicitly agree to share information with third parties.
  • For Safety and Security: To detect, prevent, or address fraud, security issues, or technical problems.
  • For Research and Analytics: Aggregated or anonymized data may be shared for insights or product improvements.

Cookies and tracking technologies help us:

  • Understand user behavior to improve our services and website experience.
  • Personalize content, offers, and recommendations based on your preferences.
  • Enable functionality like remembering login details, language, and settings.
  • Measure website traffic and performance for analytics purposes.
  • Support marketing campaigns and targeted advertisements responsibly.
  • Ensure security by detecting suspicious activity and potential threats.

You can manage cookies via your browser settings or device preferences.

You have rights regarding your data:

  • Access, update, or request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing or promotional communications.
  • Withdraw consent for certain data collection activities.

For requests, email: privacy@avasthelp.com

  • We encourage parents to monitor their children's online activity and guide them in using our services safely.
  • Children under 13 should only use our services with parental consent and supervision.
  • Any personal information collected from children will be used solely to provide requested services and will not be shared for marketing purposes.
  • Parents can request access to their child’s information or ask for it to be deleted by contacting our privacy team.
  • We maintain strict security measures to ensure that any data we may inadvertently collect from children is protected.
  • We regularly review our services to ensure compliance with child privacy regulations and best practices.

For any questions or concerns regarding child data protection, email: privacy@avasthelp.com
